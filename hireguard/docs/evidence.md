# Evidence bank

Every external figure the pitch, the business document and the sales sheets may use. A percentage that is not in this file, and not in
`eval/results/*.json`, may not be published: `python scripts/check_claims.py` enforces it.

**How the rows were checked (1 Nov 2026).** The figures and caveats were supplied by the team after opening each source. On 1 Nov the
assistant checked only that every URL responds (GlobeNewswire answers HTTP 403 to automated requests, which is usual bot-blocking, so open
it in a browser); it did **not** re-read the pages. Read the source before you quote a number to a buyer. Do not shorten the caveats.

with attacks written by our team. They may be quoted with their sample size and the words "synthetic" and "our test".
