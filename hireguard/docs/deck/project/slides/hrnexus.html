~3:30–3:55 · A. A: “Venidis is not only about hiring. HR Nexus, our Team module, helps HR with the people already inside.” A (one tile at a time): “The Answer Desk answers employees with the exact handbook passage, and sends personal questions to HR. The skills map shows which skills a team is missing, as counts, never names. Process metrics show how many days each hiring step takes, so HR sees the bottleneck. And the knowledge-gap radar shows which handbook sections employees keep asking about.” A (bottom row): “And it feeds hiring: when the data team has nobody with Spark, the gap becomes a proposed weight in the hiring rubric, and HR decides.” A: “All of it aggregated, groups under five hidden, no individual scores. And that rule comes from research.”
