B: “Before building HR Nexus we ran seven experiments, six of them on real public datasets.” B (left to right): “Skills: from five work descriptions we find the right occupation in the top five 84% of the time, across 914 O*NET occupations. Structure: in real email data, the top 1% of people carry 17.7% of cross-team links, a measurable key-person risk. Stress: on 8,463 workers, work hours predict reported stress with an AUC of only 0.64, barely better than chance.” B: “So we kept what works at team level, and dropped scoring individuals. The research decided the product.”
